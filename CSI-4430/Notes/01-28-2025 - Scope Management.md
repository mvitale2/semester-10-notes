# Scope Management
Managing scope entails communicating with all disparate parties involved in the project to coordinate its activities. But first, it must determined which parties re even involved in the project. 

A change that is out of scope should be ejected from the project so that it can be handled by another team at another time. 

It can be important to collect requirements. This can be done in a variety of ways:
- Interviewing stakeholders
- Focus groups & workshops
- Group creativity
- Surveys
- Observation studies
- Brainstorming

In many ways, it's like running a check-up on someone. A check-up involves various requirements that need to obtained by measuring someone's body, such as blood pressure or height. Determining requirements that are needed to do a "check-up" on a project can be helpful to its success. 

In Google, they do not rely on Agile or software testers. The quality of the code rests with those who write it.
