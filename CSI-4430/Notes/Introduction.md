A 1995 Standish Group Study (CHAOS) found that only 16.2% of IT projects were successful. Over 31% were cancelled before completion. 

The same study found that projects using an agile methodology:
- Had a 29% better success rate
- 39% of projects were successful compared to 11% of waterfall projects. 
# What is a project?
A project is temporary endeavor undertaken to create a unique, service, or result. A project ends when the group managing it believe they have met their objectives. 

An operation, on the other hand, is work done to sustain the business. 
## Examples of IT Projects
- Smartphone application
- Driverless cars
- A system to track child immunizations
## Attributes of a Project
A project has a...
- Fixed timeline
- Unique purpose
- Requires resources from various areas/industries
- Customer base or sponsor
## Constraints
Projects have 3 constraints: scope, cost, and time. Increasing one means sacrificing the other. 
## Stakeholders
People involved in or affected by project activities.
