The greatest threat to a project is the failure to communicate. 

Partaking in a meal with someone is a great way to form a bond and gain a positive outcome from a meeting. It helps reinforce the usage of the same resources. 

Asking someone how they would like to communicate helps them feel seen. 

Mythical Man Month

People usually prefer informal communications. 

As the number of communications channels increases, the complexity increases: $n(n-1)/2$.

Three types of communications:
- Interactive
	- Voice, text, etc.
- Push
	- Emails, voice mail, newsletters, etc.
- Pull
	- Nothing moves forward until there is input from everybody.
