Project management saves time and resources, allowing a project to reach its deliverables on-time. It does not save costs. In fact, PM might increase costs since a large majority of projects go over their expected budget. 

A **project** is a temporary endeavor undertaken to create a unique, product, service, or result. 

Projects involve **lots of uncertainty**. There are many estimates, but there is no way to know whether or not that the project will meet those estimates. 

The PMI offers the PMP (Project Management Professional) certification. 

Project work is most successful in an organizational culture where all of the following characteristics are important except **control**. 

**Scrum** is the leading agile development method. 

**Executing** processes include carrying them out. 

Which process normally requires the most *resources* and *time*? **Executing**

**Business cases** are completed before initiating a project. A business case emphasizes a strong return of value. These are not a part of project integration management. 

**Planning** outputs things like project schedules, work breakdown structures, etc.

It is not a best practice to select projects that will take less than two years to provide payback. 

A **Directive** includes a new information system project to provide data for a new government regulation. 

A **project charter** is a document that formally recognizes the existence of a project and provides direction on the project's objectives and management. 

It is not suggested to **minimize change** to perform integrated change control. 

**Interviews** are the most expensive and time consuming tool to collect requirements for a project. 

Duration and effort are not synonymous. 

Accountants usually define a **cost** as a resource sacrificed or foregone to achieve a specific objective. 

**Management** reserves allow for future situations that are unpredictable. 

The purpose of project quality management is **to ensure that the project will satisfy the needs for which the project was undertaken**. 

The term *kaizen* means **improvement**. 

A **maturity** model helps organizations improve their overall processes and systems. 

**Duration estimating** is not a part of resource management. 

**Confrontation** is a good method for conflict management when both the task and relationship are at high risk.
