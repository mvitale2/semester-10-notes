Projects involve 5 project management process groups:
- Initiating
- Planning
- Executing
- Monitoring and controlling
- Closing

Project managers that spend more time planning tend to spend less time in execution. Project managers in any field know how important it is to follow a good process. 
# Pre-initiation Tasks
You must lay the groundwork for a project before it starts:
- Determine scope, time, cost
- Identify sponsor (who's the champion?)
- Develop a business case
- Select the manager
- Meet with the team to review process and expectations for management
- Determine if project should be divided into smaller parts
