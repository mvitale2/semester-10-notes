Procurement means to acquire goods and/or services from an outside source. 

How much outsourcing is done depends on the company. Some companies do more, some do less. It is common for companies to outsource IT services, for example. This can be troublesome when the company who did the project leaves, and then all of the knowledge of the project is no longer within the company that uses it. 

Outsourcing is mostly useful for:
- Accessing skills and technologies
- Reducing both fixed and recurrent costs
- Allowing the client organization to focus on its core business
- Providing flexibility
- Increasing accountability
