# Thesis Statement
[[Learning to Live With Red Tape|"Learning to Live With Red Tape"]] was written in 1979 by Irwin Gray. He creates an argument in favor of "red tape," or, as Gray puts it, "company rules, procedures, and policies." The purpose of this essay is to determine whether or not his claims still hold 45 years later based on recent research of organizational red tape.
