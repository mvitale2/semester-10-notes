# Net Present Value Analysis
Analyzing the outcomes of investing money into developing a new product. 
## Payback Analysis
Payback period is the amount of time it will take to recoup, in the form of net cash inflows, the total dollars invested in a project. 
# Managing project knowledge
Explicit knowledge: easily explained using words, pictures, or numbers.
Tacit knowledge: difficult to express and highly personal.

Knowledge management should be done before, during, and after a project. 
# Change Control System
Formal, documented process that describes when and how official project documents and work may have changed. 
## Configuration Management
Ensures that the descriptions of the project's products are correct and complete.
