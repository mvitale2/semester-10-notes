The toughest part of an IT project is the people. This is because people are not easily replaceable; they are unpredictable, unruly, and have many different personalities. 

Agile methodology recommends groups of 3-9 people.

The best way to find new people is to improve benefits and redefining work hours and incentives. You can't just grab people off of a shelf -- they need to be motivated to work.

# Keys to Managing and Leading People
Identifying how intrinsic and extrinsic motivation can be used within the group to motivate people to work. 

## Psychology
Maslow's hierarchy of needs has been shown to help members feel motivated to work. 

Herzberg's Motivational-Hygiene Theory posits that people are more motivated by social forces, such as job titles. 

McClelland's acquired needs theory suggests that peoples' needs are shaped by their life experiences.
