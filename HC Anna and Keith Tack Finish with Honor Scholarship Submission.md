My name is Michael Vitale. I am an undergraduate senior studying Information Technology with a minor in Spanish Language. I will be taking HC 3900 during the summer along with CSI 4999, my Senior Capstone Project. 

This upcoming Fall will be my twelfth, my last, semester at Oakland University. Ever since my first semester, I have been a member of The Honors College. I have dutifully satisfied each requirement needed to remain a member. My minor in Spanish exceeded the language requirement. I exceeded the necessary volunteer hours by participating in the OU Gaming Guild nearly every Friday. Finally, I have maintained a GPA well above 3.5 for my entire college career so far, despite taking many challenging courses. I am a dedicated member of The Honors College, as well as a dedicated Golden Grizzly. 

My time at OU has been defined by consistent social growth. As a freshman, I had difficulties in social situations. I struggled with meeting new people in public, and even volunteering in class. I remembered seeing the Gaming Guild on a piece of paper listing all of the student organizations on campus. When I went to that first meeting in September, I had no idea what I was getting myself into. Now, every Friday, I come together with my newfound group of friends to play board games. 

The Guild has been the driving force behind every major social milestone during my four years here. It’s where I met my roommate, my first girlfriend, and the close-knit circle of friends I now travel with every summer. One friend I met there introduced me to the gym, sparking a routine that has since become a solo commitment. Through the shared passion for games, the Guild has helped me grow into a more well-rounded, approachable person. Now, I’m eager to give back—currently as Vice President, and soon, as President.

My professional growth at OU has been remarkable. As a freshman, I was uncertain about my career path, but over time, my direction has become clear. I’ve developed a diverse skill set across multiple programming languages and technologies, sparking my interest in software development, cybersecurity, networking, hardware, and full-stack web development. These passions led me to an internship with Macomb County Government, a technology helpdesk role on campus, and eventually an IT internship with Stellantis. Today, I have a well-defined career vision—one made possible by the support of Oakland University.

Oakland University has graciously supported me with the Talented Scholar Award; however, the financial aid only lasts for four years. This semester will be my fourth year at OU. I'm slightly behind in my courses because of my choice to complete a minor in Spanish. Now I need to spend an extra two semesters to complete my undergraduate degree. I will have to do this relying entirely on my parents' hard-earned capital. On top of this, they are supporting me in paying the rent for my apartment. My parents and I need financial support now more than ever so that I can finish my degree. 

Each time I drive down University Drive to head home, I see the sign "Seek Virtue, Knowledge." Through my social connections at the Gaming Guild I have found Virtue. Through my dedication to my studies at OU I have found Knowledge. Everything that I am now has been made possible because of Oakland University. I only need a little more support before I can conclude my journey.
