4. **List four access technologies. Classify each one as home access, enterprise access, or wide-area wireless access.**
   - WiFi: A *wide-area wireless access* technology that enables data transfer for a multitude of devices. 
   - Fiber optics: A *home and enterprise* access technology that transfers data at the speed of light. 
   - 5G: A *wide-area wireless access* technology used primarily by mobile devices like cell phones.
   - Cable broadband: A *home and enterprise* access technology which utilizes modems to provide a high-speed internet connection. 
5. **Is HFC transmission rate dedicated or shared among users? Are collisions possible in a downstream HFC channel? Why or why not?**
   HFC transmission is shared among users. Collisions are not possible because the signal is broadcast from a single source to all users simultaneously.

10. **Describe the most popular wireless Internet access technologies today. Compare and contrast them.**
    **Mesh networks** are growing in popularity for home-use. They consist of interconnected nodes that distribute internet access across a wide area. 
    **Bluetooth** is used a variety of IoT devices and computer peripherals to make connecting multiple devices easy. 
    **Cellular networks**, such 4G and 5G, are used to provide an internet connection for mobile devices. 
    **WiFi** is the most common wireless technology. It is used for local area connections as well as internet access.
    
    Each of these technologies provides a wireless connection for a vast array of devices, but each has their own specific use case. Bluetooth, for example, does not provide internet connection capabilities. Additionally, cellular networks can only be used with devices that have a SIM card.
