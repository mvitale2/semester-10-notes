The act of protecting the systems that hold and process critical data. The systems can be any asset owned by the organization:
- Laptops
- Servers
- Workstations
- Mobile devices
- Routers
- Etc
# Basic Terms
- Attack: An attempt to destroy, expose, alter, disable, steal, or gain unauthorized access to an asset. 
- Vulnerability: A weakness that allows an attacker to reduce a system's information assurance. 
- Exploit: A piece of software, data, or a sequence of commands that takes advantage of a vulnerability to cause unintended or unanticipated behavior to occur on computer software and hardware.
